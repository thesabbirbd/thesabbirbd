## 👨‍🚀 01 // The Journey & Engineering Mindset

I represent a distinctive convergence of **technical systems expertise and business strategy**. Currently pursuing a **BBA in Management at Rajshahi College**, my heart beats in the terminal. I am obsessed with architecting resilient backend systems, deploying local-first AI pipelines, and mastering modern DevOps infrastructure.

Right now, I am executing an intensive **"100-Day Backend & DevOps Engineering Mission"** — transforming architectural theory into production-grade infrastructure.

- 🎯 **Current Focus:** Backend Engineering, Microservice Containerization, and Resilient Cloud Architecture.
- 🐧 **Linux & Kernel Tuning:** Power user customizing **Ubuntu environments**, automating daily operations with modular **Bash**, and optimizing dedicated hardware.
- 🤖 **AI Tinkerer & Research:** Serving offline LLMs locally via **Ollama**, optimizing dedicated GPU VRAM allocations, and researching cutting-edge **AI-ML-XR** applications.
- 🌐 **Infrastructure & NOC Operations:** Professional **NOC Support experience**, high-uptime network diagnostics, and certified in **Cloud Engineering V1 (Muktopaath)**.
- ⚙️ **Hardware Hacker:** DIY electronics innovator building custom uninterruptible power supplies (**UPS**), multi-transistor audio amplifiers, and off-grid **solar setups**.
- 🎬 **Creative Soul:** Experienced multimedia editor, photographer, and videographer architecting complex multi-camera **OBS Studio** real-time signal routing pipelines.
