## ⚡ 01 // THE JOURNEY & MINDSET

> *"Bridging analytical business management with resilient, production-grade cloud and backend infrastructure."*

I am **Md Sabbirul Islam Khan** (known simply as **Sabbir**), an ambitious engineer operating at the strategic intersection of **backend systems, cloud infrastructure, and business management economics**. Currently pursuing my **BBA in Management at Rajshahi College**, I bring a uniquely balanced vantage point to systems architecture: understanding not only how code and networks run, but precisely how technology drives scalability, cost efficiency, and organizational impact.

* 🚀 **The 100-Day Engineering Sprint:** Currently deep in the trenches executing an intensive **"100-Day Backend & DevOps Engineering Mission"** — systematically building high-throughput APIs, container orchestration workflows, and resilient deployment pipelines.
* 🐧 **Linux & Kernel Customization:** Power user dedicated to optimizing custom **Ubuntu environments**, automating system tasks with modular **Bash scripts**, and squeezing maximum compute from dedicated hardware.
* 🤖 **Local-First AI & Offline Compute:** Exploring localized intelligence by serving offline LLMs via **Ollama**, optimizing dedicated GPU memory channels, and conducting exploratory research across **AI, ML, and XR**.
* 📊 **Data Science & Analytics:** Harnessing **Python** for granular data analysis, statistical evaluation, and interactive visualizations that inform sound architectural and operational decisions.
* 🛡️ **NOC Operations & Cloud Engineering:** Battle-tested **NOC Support Specialist** skilled in active network health monitoring and incident mitigation. Officially certified in **Cloud Engineering V1 (Muktopaath)**.
* 🔋 **DIY Hardware & Electronics:** Hardware tinkerer building off-grid **solar setups**, high-capacity uninterruptible power units (**UPS**), and custom **multi-transistor audio amplifiers**.
* 🎬 **Visual Storyteller:** Experienced multimedia director skilled in **photography, cinematography**, and architecting multi-camera **OBS Studio** real-time signal routing pipelines.
