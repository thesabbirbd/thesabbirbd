## 👨‍🚀 01 // Engineering Identity & Mindset

I am **Md Sabbirul Islam Khan**, known professionally as **SABBiR**. Operating at the strategic intersection of **business management economics and production-grade software engineering**, I blend high-level organizational vision with deep terminal-level execution.

Currently pursuing my **BBA in Management at Rajshahi College**, my engineering perspective is grounded in return on investment, operational efficiency, and scalable infrastructure design. Rather than writing code in isolation, I build systems engineered to sustain real-world business demand.

* 🎯 **Backend Systems & Architecture:** Architecting robust backend architectures, high-performance RESTful APIs, and resilient data layers.

* 🛡️ **NOC Operations & Infrastructure:** Practical **NOC Support experience** in 24/7 uptime surveillance, packet-level diagnosis, and officially certified in **Cloud Engineering V1 (Muktopaath)**.
* ⚙️ **Hardware Prototyping:** Hardware enthusiast engineering custom uninterruptible power units (**UPS**), multi-transistor audio amplifiers, and off-grid **solar architectures**.
* 🎬 **Creative Engineering:** Experienced multimedia director skilled in **cinematography, photography**, and architecting multi-camera **OBS Studio** real-time signal routing pipelines.
